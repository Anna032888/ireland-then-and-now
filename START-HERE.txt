IRELAND: THEN & NOW — the plain HTML version
============================================

This folder is a complete, ready-to-publish website. No build tools, no npm, no
framework: just .html files, one stylesheet, and two folders of images.

One small script comes with it - scroll-spy.js - which slides the green dot in
the "in this section" rail down the side of the long-form pages to the section
you are reading. Only those ten pages load it; the other 81 are plain HTML.

There are 91 pages, in two languages — 46 in English and 45 in Russian. Every
English page has a Russian twin, and the EN | RU switch in the header takes you
to the same page in the other language, wherever you are.


WHERE TO START
--------------

Open  index.html  in any browser. That is the front page.

From there:

  index.html ......... the front page
  explore.html ....... all 30 places, in one list
  history.html ....... history
  culture.html ....... culture
  geography.html ..... geography, climate and wildlife
  today.html ......... Ireland today
  plan.html .......... planning a trip
  credits.html ....... every photograph and its licence
  case-study.html .... how this site was rebuilt from the 2005 original
  sitemap.html ....... every page of the site on one page
  2005.html .......... the 2005 website's own contents page
  original.html ...... the 2005 site, introduced

Russian pages have the same names with  ru-  in front: ru-index.html,
ru-explore.html, ru-2005.html, and so on. The switch in the header moves you
between the two, and it always lands on the same page — never on the homepage,
and never on the language you are already reading.

The place pages are named  place-<name>.html  and their Russian twins
ru-place-<name>.html. There are 30 of them.


THE 2005 SITE IS STILL HERE
---------------------------

It is untouched, exactly as it was, in the  archive/  folder. The entry point
is  archive/index.htm  — note the .htm ending, because that is how it was
written in 2005. 2005.html is a contents page for all 168 of its pages in both
languages.

Nothing in that folder was modernised, repaired, renamed or deleted.


TWO FILES FOR SEARCH ENGINES
----------------------------

  sitemap.xml ....... every page, for Google and Bing
  robots.txt ........ tells them where the sitemap is

Both point at  https://anna032888.github.io/ireland-then-and-now/  — if the
site ever moves, those are the two files to edit.


HOW TO PUT IT ONLINE
--------------------

GitHub Pages, in four steps:

  1. Open the repository on GitHub and click  Add file  ->  Upload files.
  2. Drag everything INSIDE this folder into the upload box — including the
     img  and  archive  folders and the hidden  .nojekyll  file. Do not upload
     the folder itself; upload its contents.
  3. Commit the changes.
  4. In the repository: Settings -> Pages. Under Source choose
     "Deploy from a branch", pick the  main  branch and the  / (root)  folder,
     then Save.

Wait a minute or two. The site will be at
  https://<your-username>.github.io/<repository-name>/
