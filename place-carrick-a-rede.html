Carrick-a-Rede

A rope bridge and a salmon fisherman's island

CountyCounty Antrim
RegionThe Causeway Coast
SectionLandscapes

A rope bridge has crossed the thirty-metre gap to the rocky island of Carrick-a-Rede for more than three hundred and fifty years.

It was built by salmon fishermen who set their nets from the island every summer, hauling their catch and their gear across a single span with a handrail. The last commercial nets were lifted in 2002, and the bridge is now crossed by visitors instead.

It swings twenty-four metres above the sea, which is enough to make the crossing memorable. The coastal walk that leads to it, part of the Causeway Coast Path, is as good as the destination.
