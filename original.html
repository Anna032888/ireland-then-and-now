The first version

The 2005 original

This site began in 2005 as a school project: a website about Ireland, written in FrontPage, page by page, in English and Russian. That version is preserved here exactly as it was, warts and all.

The 2005 site was built the way everybody built websites then: hand-written HTML with tables for layout, fixed pixel positions, VML markup left behind by Word, GIF flags for the language switch, and a small animated cabin that took you back to the homepage from every page. It was made in English and Russian, by hand, twice.

It covers the island in enormous detail — education, sport, food, the weather, the transport system, the political system, the language, the holidays, and around thirty individual sights, each on its own page with its own photograph. It has been left untouched: all 168 pages, including the ones that no longer work.

Everything you are reading now was rebuilt from it. The colours come from the original landscape photograph; the structure of the sections follows the original menu; the cabin button in the corner of every page is that same GIF. The difference is the tooling — modern HTML and CSS, photographs with clear licensing, and a Russian version that matches the English line for line.
