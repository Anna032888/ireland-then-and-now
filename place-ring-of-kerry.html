Ring of Kerry

The classic Irish drive, 179 kilometres around

CountyCounty Kerry
RegionThe Iveragh Peninsula
SectionLandscapes

The Ring of Kerry is a 179-kilometre loop around the Iveragh Peninsula, and it has been the standard Irish tour since jaunting cars first carried visitors out from Killarney in the eighteenth century.

Most people travel clockwise through Killorglin, Glenbeigh and Cahersiveen, then over the Coomanaspig pass to Waterville and Sneem before returning through Moll's Gap. Ladies View, named for Queen Victoria's ladies-in-waiting who admired it in 1861, is the photograph everybody stops for.

The real rewards are off the main road: the Skellig Ring, Valentia Island, the stone forts of Cahergall and Leacanabuaile, and the monastic remains on Skellig Michael eleven kilometres out in the Atlantic.
