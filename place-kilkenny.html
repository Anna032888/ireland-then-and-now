Kilkenny

A medieval mile of stone and craft

CountyCounty Kilkenny
RegionThe south-east
SectionCities

Kilkenny is the smallest city in Ireland and the most medieval, arranged along a kilometre-long street that locals call the Medieval Mile, running from the cathedral to the castle.

At one end is St Canice's Cathedral, with a round tower you can climb for the best view in the county; at the other is Kilkenny Castle, home of the Butlers for six hundred years.

In between are the surviving merchant houses, the black stone of the local marble, and a design and craft tradition that turned the city into the workshop of Ireland: the Kilkenny Design Centre, the craft council and a run of workshops and studios.
