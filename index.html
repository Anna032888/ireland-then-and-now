Two versions, one journey

This site began in 2005 as a school project: hand-written pages, tables, GIF flags and a small animated cabin that took you back home. It is still here, exactly as it was — nothing was thrown away.

The pages you are reading now are the same project rebuilt with modern tools, better photographs and a Russian translation that matches the English line for line. Eleven of the places below are shown side by side with the picture the 2005 site used for them.
